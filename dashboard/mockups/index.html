Almanac font variants.

Mockup 1 is the pick, with three serif options to compare. Originals from the first round still below. Source data fetched live from asracwl.github.io/getOracle. Each is a working preview fetching the same live stats.json. All four are mobile-first — resize the window or open on your phone.

No. 01d — padelytics match

Almanac · Manrope

Same font pairing as padelytics.dk — Manrope (display) + JetBrains Mono (data). Sans, not serif. Geometric humanist with proper warmth. Hero bumped to 700 weight; italics removed since Manrope ships none. This is the candidate to beat.

padelytics font sans 700 weight hero no italics
No. 01a

Almanac · Newsreader

A refined, book-like serif. Adelle-adjacent — humanist, narrow proportions, easy on the eye at large sizes. Quietest of the three, most "magazine column."

refined humanist book
No. 01b

Almanac · Source Serif 4

Adobe's open-source workhorse. More technical / mid-century than Newsreader; tabular numbers are immaculate. A serif that doesn't try to be loud.

technical workhorse restrained
No. 01c

Almanac · Instrument Serif

High-contrast display serif. The thin verticals and dramatic curves give the headlines real presence. Sharper, more editorial — leans Vogue / NYT Opinion.

high-contrast display editorial
No. 01 (original)

Almanac · Fraunces

The original Fraunces version, for reference. The one you said felt off.

reference
No. 02

The Terminal

Phosphor-green CRT console. VT323 + JetBrains Mono, scanlines, glowing text. The chart is a Unicode-block sparkline grid (▁▂▃▄▅▆▇█). Most utilitarian and arguably the most fun.

green/black all-mono sparkline retro
No. 03

The Field Log

Light theme — warm cream paper, deep ink, Spectral + Fraunces italics. A hand-drawn SVG sparkline trace, a wax-seal status pill, and a magazine-style ledger. Most distant from today's design.

cream/ink serif sparkline light theme
No. 04

The Dial

A single polar SVG instrument as the hero: concentric rings = days, 24 segments per ring = hours, intensity = attempts. Hour bezel labels and a slow radar sweep. Bricolage Grotesque + JetBrains Mono.

teal/dark grotesk + mono polar SVG instrument
